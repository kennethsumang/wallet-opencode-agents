# Financial Health Insights

Turn calculated financial metrics into useful observations.

## Identify

Look for:

* Improving or declining net worth
* Increasing or decreasing expenses
* Changes in income
* Changes in savings
* Significant cash-flow changes
* Growing or declining debt
* Changes in investment balances
* Large account-balance changes
* Significant spending shifts
* Persistent recurring expenses

## Prioritize

Focus on the observations that are:

1. Largest in financial impact
2. Most persistent
3. Most unusual
4. Most relevant to the user's question

Do not produce a long list of minor observations.

## Language

Use concise, neutral language.

Prefer:

> "Expenses increased 14% compared with the previous period."

over:

> "Your spending is getting out of control."

Distinguish facts from interpretation.

Use:

* "The data shows..."
* "Compared with..."
* "This may indicate..."
* "A notable change is..."

Avoid unsupported claims about the user's financial behavior or future.

## Strengths

Identify measurable positive developments such as:

* Increasing savings
* Positive cash flow
* Growing net worth
* Declining debt
* Increasing investment balances

Only identify a strength when supported by the data.

## Areas to Watch

Identify measurable concerns such as:

* Persistent negative cash flow
* Increasing expenses
* Declining savings
* Increasing debt
* Large unexplained balance changes
* Significant recurring expenses

These are observations, not financial advice.

## Final Summary

End with a short summary containing:

* Overall financial position
* Most important change
* Most important trend
* One or two areas worth monitoring

Keep the final answer easy to scan.
